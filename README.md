**LANTERN THIEF — Steal the Ember Museum's Three Relics.** A complete original top-down stealth game: the museum sleeps, three ember relics glow in the dark, and four guards — Amber, Brine, Cinder, Dusk — walk their patrols with lanterns sweeping vision cones across the floor. Slip behind pedestals, crouch in deep shadow where a guard's sight shrinks, steal all three relics, and reach the EXIT unseen. Running is fast but loud: guards hear it and come to listen. A full detection meter means a chase, and a chase that catches you ends the night. Title, play, win and caught flows, a live HUD (relics, detection meter, timer, guard states), and deterministic screenshot scenes — all art drawn in code, no libraries, no network.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```

Or open `game/index.html` directly in a desktop browser. Keyboard: WASD / arrows to move, Shift to run, Enter to start, R to restart.
